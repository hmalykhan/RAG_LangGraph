NOVATECH DEMO STORE - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
CLASSROOM SAMPLE DATA ONLY. This is a fictional shop, not a real business policy.

Policy ID: RETURNS-2026-DEMO

1. DAMAGED PRODUCTS
A customer may request a return for a product that arrived damaged within 14 calendar days after delivery. A damaged laptop delivered 10 days ago is within this 14-day window. The customer should provide their order ID, a short description of the damage, and at least one clear photograph. The support team must review the request before confirming acceptance. An AI assistant must not promise an automatic refund.

2. CHANGE OF MIND
An unopened, unused product may be returned within 7 calendar days after delivery for a change-of-mind request. Opened or used products are not eligible under this rule unless they are damaged or defective.

3. REFUND PROCESS
After a return is accepted and the item passes inspection, the demo store aims to issue a refund decision within 5 business days. Do not interpret this as a guaranteed payment-arrival time. Refunds and payment transfers are not part of the student application.

4. HOW TO REQUEST HELP
Ask for the order ID, item, issue, and date of delivery. Create a support ticket through the Support Agent. The ticket status starts as OPEN. The user can later ask for the ticket's current status.

5. EXCEPTIONS
If evidence is missing, ask the customer for the missing details. If the policy does not address a special case, say that the support team needs to review it. Never invent eligibility.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS
- Can I return my damaged laptop after 10 days?
- I received a broken keyboard. What proof is required?
- Can I return an opened product because I changed my mind?
