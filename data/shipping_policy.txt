NOVATECH DEMO STORE - SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY
CLASSROOM SAMPLE DATA ONLY. This is a fictional shop, not a real business policy.

Policy ID: SHIPPING-2026-DEMO

1. PROCESSING TIME
After an order is placed, the demo store usually processes and dispatches it within 1 to 2 working days. Weekends and public holidays do not count as working days.

2. STANDARD DELIVERY
Standard delivery normally takes 3 to 5 working days after dispatch within the demo service area. This is an estimate, not a fixed guarantee. Delivery may be delayed by weather, public holidays, address problems, or courier issues.

3. TRACKING
A tracking number may be available after dispatch. The customer can ask the Support Agent to record a complaint if their delivery is late. This classroom application does not connect to an external courier API, so it must not invent live tracking positions.

4. LATE ORDERS
If more than 5 working days have passed since dispatch, ask the customer for the order ID and the date of dispatch. Create a support ticket to request an update. The ticket must use real data saved in SQLite.

5. DAMAGED PARCELS
For an item that arrives damaged, explain the return-policy process and ask for order ID and damage details. Use the separate return_policy.txt file for the exact eligibility window.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS
- How many working days does standard delivery take?
- My package is late. How can I open a complaint?
- Do you have live courier tracking?
